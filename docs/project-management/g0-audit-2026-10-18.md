## Transaction-boundary finding — blocked for producer wiring

`ERP_OPERATIONS`, `ERP_AUTOMATION`, and `ERP_TRACEABILITY` contain internal
`COMMIT` statements in business procedures. Representative routines include
stock in/out, production completion, material reservation/release, lot issue,
and traceable completion. Autonomous commits also exist only for error/audit
paths (`log_error`, automation run writer).

Therefore a caller cannot presently prove that a new outbox-row write and an
existing business write commit or roll back together. G1 creates only the
outbox storage contract; it **does not publish events and does not modify any
existing workflow**.

To unblock producer wiring, run the following against a disposable Oracle QA
schema, with deterministic concurrent requests and no ambient target:

1. Reserve the same material from two independent sessions.
2. Assert accepted reservations never exceed stock and rejected requests leave
   no physical-ledger row or outbox row.
3. Force an error after a proposed outbox insert and before final business
   completion; assert both roll back together.
4. Compare `STOCK` snapshot balances against immutable
   `INVENTORY_TRANSACTION` movements and lot balances after every run.

## Reuse boundary

FactoryFlow retains its Oracle schema and workflows. The new table is additive.
For OpsBridge, reuse only the proven *patterns* from FlashSale: port/adapter
layering, durable inbox/outbox, retry/backoff/DLQ tests, idempotency and
OpenTelemetry conventions. Do not reuse flash-sale order, payment, stock, or
customer business semantics.
