## 1. Statement of Acceptance

The undersigned stakeholder representatives hereby certify that the **MiniERP Manufacturing & Warehouse System (Release 1.1)** has successfully undergone User Acceptance Testing in accordance with the UAT Plan (`UAT-PLN-001`).

All critical functional and non-functional requirements have been verified, including:
- Inbound goods receipt with lot and expiration date tracking.
- Automated BOM explosion and atomic production order completion.
- Two-person approval gating for inventory write-offs and adjustments.
- Role-based authorization and immutable audit logging.
- Backward and forward lot genealogy tracing for quality recall.
- Fail-soft external IT Helpdesk integration.

There are **zero (0) Critical or High defects** outstanding. The system is formally accepted for production deployment and cutover.
